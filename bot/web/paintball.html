How to play

  1. Each round, pick one of your three bunkers to hide behind.
  2. Pick one of their three bunkers to shoot at.
  3. Hit FIRE. Picks stay secret until both players lock in.
  4. If your shot lands where they're hiding: splat, they're out. If you both splat each other, the round replays.
  5. Multiplayer rounds have a 25 second clock. Miss two picks in a row and you forfeit.
